# Training Research

**Status:** Workbench exploration; not authoritative

This directory contains research briefs produced by the explicitly invoked Training Research Pilot 001 skill.

Pilot 001 contains instructional-design research evidence only.

Material in this directory is:

- not Canon;
- not accepted Academy content;
- not an Academy design decision;
- not a source of authority;
- not automatically incorporated into courses or other Spark OS material.

## Pilot 001 boundary

Pilot 001 is limited to the fixed public-source instructional-design question concerning the effects, limitations, and conditions of worked examples for novice learners in professional or comparable educational settings.

Pilot 001 does not conduct:

- mortgage-domain research;
- legal or regulatory research;
- lender or insurer research;
- product research;
- underwriting research;
- qualification research;
- operational research.

Those areas remain outside Pilot 001 and require separate future review and authorization.

## Later consultation

A later Course Prototype task may consult a brief only when Sheldon explicitly names its exact path and authorizes consultation as not-authoritative research evidence.

This exact-named-brief requirement is a provisional Pilot 001 safeguard. It is not an accepted Academy, Governance, or permanent course-development protocol.

Permission to consult a brief does not:

- make it authoritative;
- approve its conclusions;
- authorize automatic incorporation;
- create an Academy decision;
- authorize modification of the brief.

## Lifecycle boundary

Research briefs do not automatically trigger:

- promotion;
- incorporation;
- downstream review;
- supersession;
- retirement;

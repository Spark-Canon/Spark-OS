**Date:** 2026-07-27

## Milestone

Spark OS installed seven accepted Principles for Spark Mortgages as durable decision rules derived from the five accepted Values.

The Principles address the basis and correction of judgment, informed client choice, conflicting benefit, accountability, readiness, and continuity. Their order supports comprehension rather than hierarchy.

## Why It Mattered

The Principles translate non-negotiable commitments into reusable guidance for recurring consequential decisions without becoming procedures, controls, metrics, or another Governance layer.

Their development also confirmed the governing Explore → Challenge → Audit → Close → Distill method at a larger scale. Candidate rules were tested independently and as a complete set, while implementation detail remained allocated downstream.

## Accepted Scope

1. Make the Basis of Judgment Clear
2. Reconsider When Evidence Warrants It
3. Support Informed Choice
4. Keep Conflicting Benefit From Distorting Judgment
5. Match Accountability to Authority and Ability
6. Match Readiness to Consequence
7. Preserve What Future Judgment Requires

## Major Milestones

### Source Inventory and Opening Architecture

The exploration reviewed foundational terms whose ambiguity could materially affect interpretation, authority, responsibility, judgment, or implementation across multiple accepted documents.

The smallest credible opening set contained three related families:

1. Authority, Accountability, and Responsibility
2. Material, Evidence, and Judgment
3. Capability, Competence, and Readiness

Knowledge and Understanding were deferred to avoid reopening earlier Knowledge exploration. Mortgage, instructional, and other domain terminology remained outside scope.

### Independent Opening Audit

The opening architecture passed independent audit with required Challenge refinements. The audit required the work to:

- preserve the accepted Values definition of Material rather than create a competing paraphrase
- test whether decision authority and repository authority genuinely shared one definition
- prevent Accountability and Responsibility from silently creating duties
- test every definition for circularity
- compare the opening set against Proportionate, Uncertainty, Basis of judgment, and Consequence

No human preference decision was required before Challenge.

### Focused Nine-Term Challenge

Each term was tested against accepted passages, boundary cases, neighbouring concepts, circularity, authority ownership, and possible additions to accepted meaning.

The Challenge concluded that:

- decision authority should remain a foundational Glossary candidate, while repository and source authority remained Governance-owned
- Accountability should describe identifiable answerability without introducing universal duties to explain, review, or respond
- Responsibility should relate to assigned role and relevant circumstances without creating universal duties to pause, refuse, escalate, or correct
- Material should become a faithful navigation entry using the accepted Values wording rather than a competing definition
- Evidence should be relational to a claim, question, judgment, or decision and remain distinct from source, fact, assertion, proof, authority, rationale, and the wider basis of judgment
- Judgment should mean context-sensitive evaluation, with professional judgment treated as a bounded application rather than a separate foundational term
- Capability, Competence, and Readiness should remain distinct, non-sequential concepts that do not imply or guarantee one another

The omission test deferred Uncertainty, left Basis of judgment with accepted Principle 1, and left Consequence in ordinary language constrained by Principle 6. Proportionate remained the only unresolved admission candidate.

### Focused Challenge on Proportionate

Proportionate was tested across evidence response, explanation, informed choice, conflicting benefit, accountability, readiness, validation, supervision, preservation, review depth, urgency, resource constraints, privacy, severe low-probability consequences, cumulative effects, reversibility, and diminishing returns.

The Challenge found that proportionality can require either escalation or restraint. It cannot be reduced to convenience, minimal effort, equal balancing, numerical thresholds, or a universal decision formula. It cannot waive binding obligations, and low probability does not neutralize catastrophic or irreversible consequences.

Navigation-only treatment was rejected because readers would otherwise need to reconstruct the same materially important cross-document relationship repeatedly. Proportionate therefore became the tenth candidate entry.

### Formal Audit and Exploration Close

Formal Audit examined the ten-entry set for necessity, fidelity to accepted sources, circularity, overlap, everyday usefulness, authority ownership, downstream boundaries, and hidden creation of duties or theories.

All entries survived with bounded refinements:

- `Authority (decision authority)` made the intended sense explicit without redefining repository authority
- Evidence recognized absence only when the missing observation, information, or result was reasonably expected under relevant conditions
- system capability was prevented from implying human competence, judgment, responsibility, accountability, or moral agency
- Competence remained dependent on applicable standards supplied by legitimate external or downstream sources
- Readiness remained situational and non-recursive
- Proportionate remained broad enough to recognize evidence, uncertainty, consequences, rights, obligations, legitimate authority, and practical constraints without becoming an exhaustive or equally weighted formula
- Material reproduced the accepted Values wording exactly as a navigation entry

The exploration closed as sufficiently tested non-authoritative Workbench source material. Closure did not approve any definition or modify the canonical Glossary.

### Candidate 001 Distill and Bounded Revision

Distill compressed the closed source material into one ten-entry candidate using the existing six-field Glossary structure: Term, Definition, Distinction, Authority, Related terms, and Status.

Structural validation confirmed ten headings, sixty required fields, resolving internal anchors, exact correspondence between Material and the accepted Values wording, and explicit non-authoritative status.

Independent audit S-113 then authorized only bounded corrections. The revision:

- converted Accountability's definition into a complete sentence
- clarified that system capability describes what a system can produce under specified conditions
- removed wording that could imply the Competence entry granted general permission for supported participation
- compressed Proportionate's distinction while retaining the audited safeguards

## Alternatives and Concerns

The exploration rejected:

- a comprehensive ontology or definition of every recurring word
- one abstract definition spanning both decision authority and repository authority
- a separate Professional judgment entry
- a competing Glossary definition of Material
- universal action duties hidden inside Accountability or Responsibility
- a developmental ladder from Capability through Competence to Readiness
- a Proportionate score, threshold, formula, equal-balancing rule, or convenience default
- additional entries justified only by frequent recurrence

The primary risks were creating another authority layer, silently introducing duties or permissions through definitions, making technical systems appear to possess human agency, and allowing a foundational vocabulary to expand into an ontology, maturity model, theory, or implementation standard.

## Architectural Principles Discussed

- A foundational Glossary should clarify accepted Philosophy and Governance without deriving or changing them.
- Existing accepted definitions should be linked or reproduced faithfully rather than paraphrased into competing authority.
- Closely related terms may require different authority owners.
- Definitions must be tested for hidden duties, permissions, and normative overreach.
- Human and system capability language requires explicit agency and accountability boundaries.
- Materiality identifies potential significance; proportionality governs the kind or degree of response.
- Proportionality can require more action or less action and cannot waive binding obligations.
- Exploration, closure, distillation, and audit do not create canonical authority.

These principles are historical observations unless confirmed by the authoritative documents linked below.

## Outcome

The conversation closed Foundational Glossary Exploration 001 as non-authoritative source material and produced a revised ten-entry candidate containing nine substantive definitions plus one Material navigation entry.

No definition was approved, installed, merged, or made authoritative during the summarized conversation. The canonical [Spark OS Glossary](../../../01-philosophy/glossary.md) remained unchanged on `main` at that endpoint.

A separate installation branch was created later after reported human approval, but it remained pending independent verification and merge when this archive record was prepared. That unmerged branch does not establish a current authoritative outcome.

## Lessons Learned

- Define only terms whose ambiguity materially threatens interpretation across accepted sources.
- Preserve accepted wording instead of creating a competing paraphrase.
- Test definitions for duties and permissions they might introduce indirectly.
- Keep decision authority distinct from repository and source authority.
- Keep Capability, Competence, and Readiness distinct and non-sequential.
- Treat proportionality as a two-direction discipline capable of escalation or restraint.
- Use formal Audit to fix wording architecture without reopening settled exploration.
- Distill under fixed constraints rather than restarting conceptual discovery.
- Bound independent revision to explicit findings and verify it through fingerprints and structural checks.
- Never treat a historical checkout as current repository authority.
